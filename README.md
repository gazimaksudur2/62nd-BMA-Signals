## Qualifying-mark strategy

- **Technical (100 marks, qualifying 40):** You need 40%. Focus on networking (highest weight) and C programs, which together have covered 40–50 marks in every past paper reviewed. Even writing a partial but correct OSI-layer answer plus a working Fibonacci program gets you very close to qualifying.
- **GK (50 marks, qualifying 20–25):** You need 40–50%. Negative marking of −0.5 per wrong MCQ applies in at least some sittings. Do not guess blindly. The 7 Bir Sreshtho, flag facts, and a dozen common abbreviations are "free marks" — memorise them first.
