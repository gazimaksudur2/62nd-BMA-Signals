# Viva Cheat-Sheet — 60th BMA Batch Notes

> This appendix is based on merged viva experiences shared by 60th batch candidates. It is a thin supplement to the written exam prep — not the main study material.

---

## Quick-answer bank for common viva questions

### Self-introduction
Prepare a 60-second introduction covering: name, university, department, CGPA, district, and one technical interest/project. Practice out loud.

### Technical 1-liners

| Question | One-line answer |
|---|---|
| What is IPv4? | 32-bit logical addressing for identifying devices on a network; e.g. `192.168.1.1`. |
| What is IPv6? | 128-bit successor to IPv4; e.g. `2001:db8::1`; solves address exhaustion. |
| What is phishing? | A social engineering attack that tricks users into revealing sensitive credentials via fake emails/websites. |
| What is a DDoS attack? | A Distributed Denial of Service attack floods a target with traffic from many machines (botnet), making it unavailable. |
| What is modulation? | Changing a carrier signal's amplitude, frequency, or phase to encode information for transmission. |
| What is multiplexing? | Combining multiple signals onto one channel so they share the medium efficiently. |
| TCP vs UDP (one line each)? | TCP: connection-oriented, reliable, ordered. UDP: connectionless, fast, no delivery guarantee. |
| What is a firewall? | A security system that monitors and filters network traffic based on predefined rules. |
| What is an OS kernel? | Core component of the OS running in privileged mode; manages processes, memory, devices, and system calls. |

### Brain teasers (commonly asked)

**Teaser 1:** A man walks 1 km south, 1 km east, 1 km north, and is back at the starting point. Where could he be?  
→ At the **North Pole** (also at certain points near the South Pole).

**Teaser 2:** You have a 3-litre jug and a 5-litre jug. How do you measure exactly 4 litres?  
→ Fill 5L → pour into 3L → discard 3L → pour remaining 2L into 3L jug → fill 5L again → pour from 5L into 3L until full (needs 1L) → 5L jug now holds **4 litres**.

**Teaser 3:** What has hands but cannot clap?  
→ A **clock**.

## Famous people from notable districts (prepare your own)

For "name three famous people from your hometown" — prepare in advance based on your district. General examples:

| District | Notable person |
|---|---|
| Gopalganj | Sheikh Mujibur Rahman (Father of the Nation) |
| Dhaka | Many — Rabindranath Tagore (born Shelaidaha area), Kazi Nazrul Islam (Churulia, West Bengal) |
| Faridpur | Bijoy Kishor Dey (freedom fighter) |
| Sylhet | Shah Jalal (Sufi saint) |
| Chittagong | Sultan Muhammad Mansur Ali |

> **Tip:** The viva board expects honesty. If you do not know a famous person from your district, say so clearly and mention what the district is known for (e.g., agriculture, history, a river, or an industry).
