# English & Analytical Ability — GK Study Notes

> **Why it is asked:** The GK section tests basic language skills alongside factual knowledge. English questions in BMA-style exams are typically at SSC/HSC level — error correction, appropriate prepositions/conjunctions, and common idioms.

---

## 1. Common Errors and Corrections

Correct the error in each sentence.

| Incorrect sentence | Correct sentence | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| He is more better than his brother. | He is **better** than his brother. | Never use "more" with a two-syllable comparative (-er form). |
| She does not knows the answer. | She does not **know** the answer. | After auxiliary "does/do/did", use base form of verb. |
| I am agree with you. | I **agree** with you. | "Agree" is not used with the verb "be". |
| He went to home. | He went **home**. | "Home" as destination needs no preposition. |
| The news are bad today. | The news **is** bad today. | "News" is uncountable — singular verb. |
| Neither of them are ready. | Neither of them **is** ready. | "Neither" takes singular verb. |
| He is a university educated man. | He is **a** university educated man. | Correct — "university" starts with /j/ sound, so "a" not "an". |
| I have been living here since five years. | I have been living here **for** five years. | "For" with a duration; "since" with a point in time. |
| Please advice me what to do. | Please **advise** me what to do. | "Advice" is noun; "advise" is verb. |
| He is suffering from fever since Monday. | He has been suffering from fever since Monday. | Use present perfect continuous for an ongoing situation from a past time point. |

## 2. Prepositions — Common Usage

| Sentence | Correct preposition | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| He was born ___ 1990. | **in** | Years: use "in" |
| She arrived ___ Monday. | **on** | Days of the week: use "on" |
| The meeting starts ___ 9 AM. | **at** | Clock times: use "at" |
| He has been working ___ 2015. | **since** | Point in time: use "since" |
| We waited ___ three hours. | **for** | Duration: use "for" |
| He is interested ___ science. | **in** | interested **in** |
| She is good ___ mathematics. | **at** | good **at** |
| He was absent ___ the meeting. | **from** | absent **from** |
| She congratulated him ___ his success. | **on** | congratulate **on** |
| I am proud ___ my country. | **of** | proud **of** |

## 3. Fill in the Blanks — Conjunctions and Articles

| Sentence | Answer |
|---|---|
| He works hard, ___ he does not succeed. | **yet / but** |
| She is intelligent ___ hardworking. | **and** |
| I will go ___ you permit me. | **if** |
| He finished the task ___ it was difficult. | **though / although** |
| He is ___ honest man. | **an** (vowel sound /ɒ/) |
| She is ___ best student in the class. | **the** (superlative) |
| This is ___ unique opportunity. | **a** (/j/ sound) |

## Q&A Bank

**Q1.** Choose the correct preposition: "He is good ___ playing chess."  
(a) in  (b) at  (c) on  (d) for  
→ **(b)** at

**Q2.** Identify the error: "The committee have decided to postpone the meeting."  
→ "have" → **has** (collective noun "committee" takes singular verb in formal usage; acceptable as plural in British English, but singular is preferred in formal writing.)

**Q3.** Fill in: "She worked hard, ___ she passed the exam."  
(a) but  (b) so  (c) yet  (d) although  
→ **(b)** so (cause → result)

**Q4.** What does the idiom "burning the midnight oil" mean?  
→ Working or studying late into the night.

**Q5.** Correct the sentence: "He gaved me a book yesterday."  
→ "He **gave** me a book yesterday." (past tense of "give" is irregular: give → gave)
