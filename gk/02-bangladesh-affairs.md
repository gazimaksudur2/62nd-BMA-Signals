# Bangladesh Affairs — GK Study Notes

> **Why it is asked:** GK Bangladesh questions appear in every BMA paper. The 36th paper asked for the national anthem (10 marks) and 7 Bir Sreshtho (5 marks). National symbols, Liberation War milestones, geography, and Armed Forces Day are consistently tested.

---

## 1. National Symbols

| Symbol | Details |
|---|---|
| National flag | Green background, red circle offset slightly towards the hoist. Ratio **10:6** (length:width). Designer: **Kamrul Hasan** (original design); formally adopted 17 January 1972. |
| National anthem | "Amar Sonar Bangla" (আমার সোনার বাংলা) — written by **Rabindranath Tagore**. First 10 lines used as anthem. |
| National emblem | Water lily (shapla) floating on water, paddy on both sides, three jute leaves above, two stars on each side. |
| National flower | Shapla (White Water Lily) |
| National bird | Doel (Oriental Magpie-Robin) |
| National fish | Ilish (Hilsa) |
| National fruit | Jackfruit (Kathal) |
| National tree | Mango tree (Aam gach) |
| National sport | Kabaddi |

## 2. National Martyrs' Memorial (Jatiyo Smriti Soudho)

- **Location:** Savar, Dhaka (about 35 km from Dhaka city)
- **Architect:** **Syed Mainul Hossain**
- **Dedicated to:** Martyrs of the 1971 Liberation War
- **Structure:** Seven triangular towers symbolising the seven phases of the independence movement

## 3. First Lines of the National Anthem

*Amar Sonar Bangla* — for 10-mark exam questions, know the full first verse (first 10 lines in Bengali) and at least the English meaning of the first few lines:

```
আমার সোনার বাংলা, আমি তোমায় ভালোবাসি
চিরদিন তোমার আকাশ, তোমার বাতাস, আমার প্রাণে বাজায় বাঁশি।
ও মা, ফাগুনে তোর আমের বনে ঘ্রাণে পাগল করে,
মরি হায়, হায় রে—
ও মা, অঘ্রানে তোর ভরা ক্ষেতে আমি কী দেখেছি মধুর হাসি।
```

**English translation (first two lines):**  
"My golden Bengal, I love you. Your sky, your air forever play music in my heart."

## 4. The 7 Bir Sreshtho

The highest military honour of Bangladesh, awarded posthumously to seven freedom fighters of the 1971 Liberation War:

| Name | Branch |
|---|---|
| 1. Mohiuddin Jahangir | Army (Captain) |
| 2. Hamidur Rahman | Army (Sepoy) |
| 3. Mostafa Kamal | Army (Sepoy) |
| 4. Mohammad Ruhul Amin | Navy (Engine Room Artificer) |
| 5. Matiur Rahman | Air Force (Flight Lieutenant) |
| 6. Munshi Abdur Rouf | Army (Lance Naik) |
| 7. Nur Mohammad Sheikh | Army (Lance Naik) |

**Memory tip:** "MH MK MR MR MN" — Mohiuddin, Hamidur, Mostafa (Kamal), Mohammad (Ruhul Amin), Matiur (Rahman), Munshi (Abdur Rouf), Nur Mohammad.

## 5. 1971 Liberation War — Key Milestones

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 7 March 1971 | Sheikh Mujibur Rahman's historic speech at Race Course Maidan (now Suhrawardy Udyan), Dhaka — effectively a call to arms: *"Ebarer sangram amader muktir sangram"* |
| 25 March 1971 | Operation Searchlight — Pakistani military crackdown begins; start of the genocide |
| 26 March 1971 | **Independence Day** — Sheikh Mujibur Rahman (or Major Ziaur Rahman via radio) formally declared independence of Bangladesh |
| 10 April 1971 | Proclamation of Independence; Mujibnagar Government formed in exile |
| 17 April 1971 | Mujibnagar Government sworn in (at Baidyanathtala, Meherpur — renamed Mujibnagar) |
| 16 December 1971 | **Victory Day** — Pakistani forces surrender; joint command of Mukti Bahini and Indian forces accepted surrender in Dhaka |
| Total duration | 9 months of war (March–December 1971) |
