## Q&A Bank

**Q1.** Who is the current Secretary-General of the United Nations?  
→ **António Guterres** (Portugal); second term ends 31 December 2026.

**Q2.** Where is the headquarters of WHO?  
→ **Geneva, Switzerland**

**Q3.** What is the capital of Australia?  
→ **Canberra** (common trap — not Sydney or Melbourne)

**Q4.** How many permanent members does the UN Security Council have?  
→ **5** (USA, UK, France, Russia, China)

**Q5.** Who won the 2025 Nobel Peace Prize?  
→ **María Corina Machado** (Venezuela) for promoting democratic rights.

**Q6.** When did World War II end?  
→ Europe: 8 May 1945; Pacific/Global: 2 September 1945.

**Q7.** What is the currency of Japan?  
→ **Yen (JPY)**

**Q8.** Where is SAARC headquartered?  
→ **Kathmandu, Nepal**
